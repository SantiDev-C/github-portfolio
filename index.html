I focus on game development with an emphasis on mechanics and systems design. I enjoy the process of turning an idea on paper into something playable, iterating fast and paying close attention to the player experience.

My current project is CivilDeck: a strategy deck-builder where cards represent decisions that shape your civilization. I work across the full stack: code, design, UI and pixel art.
